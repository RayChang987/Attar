## Global routing (GROV)

`d.gr_overflow()`: RUDY demand per gcell edge and direction against OpenROAD's gcell grid and edge capacities, GROV = share of gcell edges whose demand exceeds capacity. Truth: OpenROAD's capacity per layer (its congestion report) and its routed usage per edge after global routing (`write_global_route_segments`) of the placement ORFS routed, at ORFS's layer adjustment (fresh) and at 0.7 (stress) (`tools/validate_gr.py`). RUDY is demand before routing: the router's detours lower the overflow of mildly congested designs and spread that of heavily congested ones.
