## Timing (STA)

Setup / hold slack of every endpoint (register data pins, outputs) against OpenSTA after `estimate_parasitics -placement`, at the ORFS 3_3_place_gp placement (`tools/validate_sta.py`) or an Attar placement exported as DEF (NS3K, `tools/validate_sta_ns3k.py`). Attar puts each pin where `estimate_parasitics` does: OpenROAD's pin location, the mean of the centers of its LEF rectangles truncated to the database unit (not the center of their bounding box, which differs on most multi-rectangle ASAP7 and Nangate45 pins). The residuals: nangate45 ibex, one clock-gate latch D pin (latches are not modeled), all its other endpoints within 0.11 ps; riscv32i-mock-sram, up to 0.3 ps at SRAM macro pins (a design's own macros keep the box center).

### Debuffer

`d.debuffer()` at the ORFS 3_3_place_gp placement removes the buffers and double inversions ORFS put in (45991 buffers, 83 inverters over 10 designs); OpenSTA reads the DEF Attar exports with the run's LEFs (`tools/validate_sta.py --debuffer`; `--via-def` is the same path without the edit). The nets grow: loads that a buffer tree split come back onto one net. A DEF does not carry the routing layers the run's odb has, so the truth sets them (`set_routing_layers -signal`, the run's): `estimate_parasitics` hangs every pin off its tree through the mean cut resistance between them, the middle of the stack without them.

## Global routing (GROV)

`d.gr_overflow()`: RUDY demand per gcell edge and direction against OpenROAD's gcell grid and edge capacities, GROV = share of gcell edges whose demand exceeds capacity. Truth: OpenROAD's capacity per layer (its congestion report) and its routed usage per edge after global routing (`write_global_route_segments`) of the placement ORFS routed, at ORFS's layer adjustment (fresh) and at 0.7 (stress) (`tools/validate_gr.py`). RUDY is demand before routing: the router's detours lower the overflow of mildly congested designs and spread that of heavily congested ones.
